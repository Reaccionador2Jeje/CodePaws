# AtDev Billing — Hybrid plans (CodePaws)

**Status:** proposal only. CodePaws remains a free, open-source terminal pet simulator.

## Hybrid model

Keep adopting, caring for, playing with, and locally customizing pets free. Optional paid offerings may be limited to voluntary support, downloadable cosmetic theme packs, or community-created add-ons if a safe distribution mechanism is implemented. Never sell gameplay advantages or make the basic experience expire.

## Proposed tiers (CLP/month; exploratory)

| Plan | Reference price | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Community | $0 | Full core simulator |
| Supporter | Voluntary | Optional contribution and supporter acknowledgment |
| Creator | $490 | Future optional theme/add-on publishing conveniences |

No paid feature is currently implemented. Any supporter benefits must be cosmetic or acknowledgment-only and must not affect core functionality.

No checkout is active. Real-money features require an adult account owner's review of payment-provider eligibility and legal/tax obligations.
